Scenario 1: Mains Electricity Connected

The 5V Adapter powers the ESP32 through Schottky diode 1N5822 and MT3608 boost module (5.10V output). The 5V rail pulls up the P-MOSFET gate, holding the MOSFET completely OFF. The 18650 battery is completely isolated from the load and charges peacefully through the TP4056 module.
